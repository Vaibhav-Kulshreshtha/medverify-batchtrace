# Bundled assets

The application loads the English OCR model and its WebAssembly worker from `public/vendor/ocr`. Tesseract.js 7.0.0, its LSTM core and the English language data run locally. The gzip model has SHA-256 `384e37bf0f5ddcc4a1e24802322a837e0b5692e18150e6078ff1d4379b191f98`. The included license and bundle notices are alongside the corresponding files. [Tesseract.js](https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js) · [Tesseract.js core](https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js-core) · [English model family](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast)

The decoder worker uses ZXing JS 0.21.3 for common QR and barcode formats and jsQR 1.4.0 as a QR fallback. An ordinary consumer GTIN remains a decoded identifier. Supported field payloads include labelled strings, string-valued JSON pack fields and a parenthesized GS1 (10) lot element. URLs are displayed as inert text. The app has no external GTIN registry or serial-number authentication service. Each vendored library includes its license. [ZXing](https://github.com/zxing-js/library) · [jsQR](https://github.com/cozmo/jsQR)

The instructional image in `public/images/pack-guide.webp` was generated with built-in ImageGen and optimized for local display. It is an illustration. The PNGs used for OCR testing are separately rendered labels. The guide prompt requested a teal-ended ivory Velmora medicine carton with a visible batch panel, manufacturing and expiry dates, a silver blister strip and a pale mint studio background. Its displayed packaging is fictional.

The three fictional sample labels can be regenerated with `scripts/make-samples.py`. The original official reference PDFs are identified and fingerprinted in the [source notes](SOURCES.md). The two photographic test inputs are attributed in [fixture licenses](../tests/fixtures/ATTRIBUTION.md) and are excluded from the production site.

## ImageGen guide brief

> Use case: product-mockup. Create a polished clinical product still-life illustration for a medicine packaging scanner. Wide landscape 3:2, solid uniform very pale cool mint backdrop (#e0efea), NO frame, NO interface, NO external typography or annotations. One long low elegant matte ivory pharmaceutical folding carton with a deep blue-green teal end cap on left and thin complementary teal stripe across white lower face. Camera at a three-quarter angle, long axis rises modestly toward right, top and long front label face visible, believable small printed technical text. On top simple fictional brand 'Velmora' and small 'TABLETS 25 mg'. On front print distinct crisp dark monospaced batch panel 'BATCH  00742A', 'MFG  01/2025', 'EXP  12/2028', small barcode. One silver blister strip of several white round tablets on its foil in foreground slightly overlapping the carton lower right and casting directional soft shadow on pastel surface. Balanced editorial studio product render with fine tactile material, sophisticated, diffuse natural upper left lighting. Center group filling 80% of canvas with extra breathing space horizontally. No extra objects, no hand, no overlay, no real brands. Photorealistic stylized 3D render.
